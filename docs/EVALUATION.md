# Evaluation Plan

## Retrieval
Precision@K, Recall@K, MRR, MAP, evidence recall, jurisdiction-filter accuracy.

## Generation
Groundedness/faithfulness, answer relevance, citation correctness/completeness, unsupported-claim rate, conflict handling.

## Classification
Accuracy, macro-F1, abstention accuracy, calibration.

## Safety
Safe-abstention precision/recall, prompt-injection success, poisoning detection, privacy leakage, restricted-source violations.

## Multilingual
Meaning preservation, legal obligation preservation, terminology accuracy, citation preservation, human reviewer score.

## System
First-token latency, end-to-end latency, retrieval latency, cost/query, cache hit rate, freshness lag, update propagation time.

Do not publish target numbers as measured results until the benchmark has actually been executed.
